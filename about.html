About Mahlodi Bakehouse

Mahlodi Bakehouse is an independent bakery based in Pretoria, South Africa. It is a welcoming spot where fresh bread, pastries, cakes and custom treats come to life for individuals, families and small businesses alike. We deliver baked goods that feel like home for anyone who stops by.

What We Stand For

Our mission

To offer affordable and high-quality baked products while making every visit feel like a friendly welcome. We believe quality and affordability go hand in hand and that a smile on a customer's face is the reward.

Our vision

To become a well-known bakery that people trust for quality products, friendly service and custom orders, a place people love to return to for every celebration and everyday treat.

Our values

  • Quality: fresh ingredients, baked daily.
  • Affordability: fair prices for students, families and offices.
  • Warmth: every customer is welcomed like family.
  • Community: supporting Pretoria events and celebrations.

Who We Bake For

Students and young adults, families, residents, office workers, customers planning events, customers interested in customised cakes and businesses that need baked products.
