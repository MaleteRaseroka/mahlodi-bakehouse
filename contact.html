Frequently Asked Questions

      

      
Do you deliver?
Yes. We deliver within Pretoria for R80 per order. Choose the delivery option on the enquiry form.

      
How far ahead must I order a custom cake?
Custom cakes need at least 7 days notice. Celebration cakes need 3 days.

      
Can you cater for allergies?
Tell us about any allergies in your message. We bake with nuts, wheat, eggs and dairy, so we cannot guarantee allergen-free products.

      
Do you take payments online?
Not yet. You pay when you collect or receive your order.
